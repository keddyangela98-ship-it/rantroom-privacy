RantRoom Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

Overview

RantRoom is a venting and reflection app. What you type in Vent is never saved. Optional Talk Me Off the Ledge and Community features use online services. RantRoom does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Information stored on your device

What you type in Vent is not saved anywhere. Mood choices, app preferences, and joke favorites are stored in local app storage on your device. Community profile credentials are also stored locally so you can stay signed in. Local app storage is not a separate encrypted vault and may be included in device backups.

Talk Me Off the Ledge

When you use Talk Me Off the Ledge, the conversation text you submit is sent over the internet to RantRoom's AI service and processed by OpenAI to generate a reply. Do not include information you would not want processed by those services. The conversation is kept in memory on your screen only while the chat is open and is not saved by the app.

Community information

If you join Community, RantRoom's Community service stores your chosen nickname, a pseudonymous profile identifier, authentication and recovery-code hashes, a pseudonymous RevenueCat app-user identifier for subscription verification, your posts and replies, reports, blocks, and timestamps. Community posts and replies are visible to other Community members and are not private rants. Do not post personal or sensitive information. Recovery codes can reconnect a profile and should be kept private.

Subscriptions

Apple or Google processes subscription payments and controls billing and cancellation. RevenueCat processes purchase and subscription-status information so RantRoom can enable Community, Talk Me Off the Ledge, and After Dark for active subscribers. RantRoom does not receive or store payment-card details.

Service providers and sharing

RantRoom does not sell your data and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. Talk Me Off the Ledge text is processed by OpenAI to generate replies. Community content is handled by the configured Community host. Hosting providers may process technical connection information in service logs. Community does not receive anything you type in Vent.

Retention and deletion

Rants are not stored, so there is nothing to delete; closing the screen discards what you typed. You can delete your Community profile from the Community screen. Profile deletion removes the profile from the active service and clears or hides your own posts and replies. Replies written by other members in conversations you contributed to and copies in service backups may remain until their retention periods end. Removing the app does not delete server-held Community data.

Security

Community credentials are stored in local app storage, which is not a separate encrypted vault. The Community service stores hashes of authentication and recovery credentials, not the raw values. Use a device you control and keep recovery codes private.

Safety

RantRoom is a release and reflection tool, not therapy, medical care, diagnosis, or emergency support. Its crisis-language warning is a basic keyword-based safety feature and may miss important situations. If you may harm yourself or someone else, contact local emergency services or an appropriate crisis service in your area.

Adult content

After Dark is an optional humor section intended for adults. The age confirmation is a content gate and is not identity verification.

Children

RantRoom is not designed for children. Store age ratings and target-audience settings should reflect the app's content before release.

Changes to this policy

This policy should be checked against the final AI provider, Community host, hosting logs, retention settings, and store build. It will be updated before any material change to data practices.
